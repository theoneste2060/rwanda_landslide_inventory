About Us

Welcome to our web application dedicated to predicting landslides using advanced Artificial Intelligence algorithms. Our application utilizes a range of environmental and geographical data to assess the likelihood of landslides occurring in the Ngororero district of Rwanda.

Our predictive model analyzes various factors including errain slope, rainfall intensity, soil type, orientation, rainfall duration, freque ncy, soil texture, permeability, porosity, moisture content, compaction, deforestation rate, infrastructure development, vegetation density, land use changes, geological composition, previous landslide history, vegetation cover, water seepage, elevation, slope aspect, land relief, l andform type, temperature, humidity, wind speed, atmospheric pressure, precipitation type, longitude, and latitude. These parameters are obtain ed through APIs and are passed to the trained model to determine landslide probability.

Our research focuses specifically on the Ngororero district in Rwanda, where the application is dedicated to automating the monitoring of landsli des. The Ngororero district faces significant challenges related to landslide occurrences, making it crucial to develop effective predictive too ls for disaster management and mitigation.

This project is owned and managed by Niyibizi Jean Pierre, the founder of GEO-HOME. GEO-HOME operates at Gihara-Ruyenzi Road in Kamonyi district, and our team is committed to leveraging technology to address environmental challenges and enhance disaster preparedness in Rwanda and beyond.
