***General Introduction*** 
This dataset contains landslide inventory and groundwater level data collected as part of PhD research project. These data are being published to serve as assets and/or supplementary material for publication of both the paper and PhD thesis of Judith Uwihirwe. Additionally, these data are being made public for reviewers cross checking and other researchers to use them in their own work.
***Dataset description and naming convention ***
The groundwater data in this dataset were collected from 2016-2021  by the Rwanda Water Resources Board and can also be freely accessed at https://waterportal.rwb.rw/data/ground water. The landslide inventory contains data from 2006-2021 and was compiled by J.Uwihirwe following the available standard methods and naming adopted by other landslide researchers e.g Monsieurs et al., 2018;  Kirschbaum et al., 2009.
